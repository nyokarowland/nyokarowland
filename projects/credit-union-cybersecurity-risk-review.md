# Credit Union Cybersecurity Risk Review

**Project type:** Academic cybersecurity risk-management analysis  
**Scenario:** Workers Werks Credit Union (WWCU), a course-provided organization

## Project overview

I evaluated WWCU’s existing IT security risk-management plan and identified gaps that could make it harder to assess enterprise-wide risk, assign responsibility, prepare for service disruptions, and meet legal and ethical obligations.

The analysis found that the plan focused mainly on individual projects, lacked clear ownership and evidence requirements for risk treatment, and did not define recovery objectives or detailed incident-reporting procedures. It also called for stronger consideration of third-party risks and the effects an incident could have on members and other stakeholders.

## Recommendations

- Expand risk assessments to cover critical services, data, users, cloud systems, suppliers, and recovery needs.
- Track each risk with an accountable owner, controls, due date, testing evidence, residual risk, and approval.
- Complete a business impact analysis and define recovery time, recovery point, backup, and restoration responsibilities.
- Map applicable legal and regulatory duties to controls, evidence, owners, and reporting steps.
- Practice incident response, recovery, and communications, and document member privacy and harm considerations.

## Sources and professional considerations

The analysis refers to NIST SP 800-30, NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, ISO 31000, GLBA, NCUA Part 748, Michigan’s Identity Theft Protection Act, and other sources listed in the academic report. The recommendations also consider professional ethics, privacy, and avoiding harm.

## What this demonstrates

Risk assessment · Control-gap analysis · Risk treatment · Business impact and recovery planning · Supply-chain risk · Legal and ethical analysis · Technical communication

## Project limits

This is an academic analysis of a course-provided scenario. It is not an assessment of a real credit union and does not contain member or customer information.
